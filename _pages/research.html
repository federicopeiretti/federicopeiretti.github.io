Research Projects (Participation)

2025 – 2026

DESPITE FRAUD

Computer Science Dept., University of Turin – Turin, Italy

Development of a portable decision-support platform combining non-destructive Near-Infrared (NIR) and Raman spectroscopy with machine learning for food authenticity and fraud detection. Key applications include identifying fresh versus thawed fish and detecting mechanically separated meat in products.

Funding: Funded under the P.R. FESR 2021/27 SWIch program (Support of the Whole Innovation Chain).
Partners & Collaborators: Politecnico di Torino, Istituto Zooprofilattico Sperimentale del Piemonte, Liguria e Valle d'Aosta (IZS PLV), Esselunga, Amadori, Informatica System Srl, Asseltech Srl.
2024 – 2026

TOPML – Trading off Non-Functional Properties of Machine Learning

Johannes Gutenberg University – Mainz, Germany

Research on trade-offs among non-functional properties of Machine Learning, with a focus on algorithmic fairness, transparency, and privacy in representation learning and unsupervised learning.

Research director: Prof. Dr. Stefan Kramer.
Funding: Funded by the Carl Zeiss Foundation.
2015 – 2023

Social4School

Computer Science Dept., University of Turin – Turin, Italy

Social4School is a web application that allows primary and secondary school students to experience typical social network dynamics through an interactive, realistic simulation. Under the supervision of the teacher, students are placed in a small artificial social graph. Throughout the game, they can post sentences with different levels of sensitivity (in terms of privacy, discrimination, hate speech). The goal of this project is to raise young people's awareness of the mechanisms underlying interaction and information sharing on online social networks.

Research directors: Prof. Ruggero G. Pensa, Dr. Sara Capecchi.
Funding: Funded by the Fondazione CRT.
